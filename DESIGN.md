## Brand & Style

The brand personality centers on seasoned authority, unshakeable safety, and rapid-response dependability. Serving residential, commercial, and industrial facilities in Puebla, Mexico for over three decades, the visual presence balances master-electrician technical precision with an approachable, transparent emergency response aesthetic.

The visual style blends **Corporate / Modern** reliability with structured, technical clarity. It rejects decorative clutter in favor of legible metrics, certifications, clear diagnostic breakdowns, and instantaneous emergency dispatch triggers. The aesthetic evokes the grounded durability of industrial electrical enclosures combined with modern, accessible utility: crisp high-contrast surfaces, decisive callout badges, and immediate pathways to assistance.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate sense of competence, safety, and operational urgency:

- **Primary (`#0F2942`)**: Deep electric navy. Serves as the bedrock of the identity, anchoring headers, primary action surfaces, trust banners, and core chrome. Reflects institutional stability, high-voltage expertise, and industrial discipline.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`)**: Vibrant electric amber. Used for high-voltage accents, warning states, and high-urgency electrical emergency CTAs (e.g., "Urgencia 24 Horas", "Solicitar Diagnóstico Inmediato"). Supported by `#EA580C` for hover and active states.
- **Tertiary (`#22C55E`)**: WhatsApp & Certified Green. Reserved strictly for real-time customer touchpoints, WhatsApp emergency triggers, guarantee checkmarks, and operational status indicators ("Técnico Disponible").
- **Neutral (`#64748B`)**: Slate neutral series. Complemented by `#F8FAFC` for base canvas backgrounds, `#FFFFFF` for elevated structural cards, and `#0F172A` for high-legibility body text. Avoid pure uncalibrated blacks; slate tones preserve technical clarity without harshness.

## Typography

The typography strategy leverages **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headers to impart geometric authority and approachable strength, paired with **Inter** for all functional, data, and body elements to deliver maximum legibility under rapid-scanning conditions.

- **Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Display weights are held at `700` and `800` to ground emergency messaging and institutional credibility. Tight letter-spacing keeps headings punchy on mobile screens during urgent queries.
- **Body & Technical Copy (`Inter`)**: Set with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to ensure technical diagnostic explanations, safety checklists, and pricing breakdowns remain effortless to comprehend under high-stress domestic or industrial failure scenarios.
- **Labels & Badges (`Inter`)**: All uppercase status labels (e.g., "SERVICIO 24/7", "CERTIFICACIÓN CFE") employ tracking of `0.05em` to `0.08em` at medium to bold weights, ensuring instant visibility on dense dispatch dashboards or floating quick-contact bars.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a strict **8pt base grid** with a 12-column responsive fluid grid framework, engineered for quick scannability and reliable touch target allocation:

- **Breakpoints**:
  - `sm` (Mobile): `360px` to `639px`, 4 columns, `16px` outer margin, `16px` gutters. Single-column stacked layouts for rapid service selection and floating emergency dialer.
  - `md` (Tablet): `640px` to `1023px`, 8 columns, `24px` outer margin, `20px` gutters. Two-column service and pricing matrix.
  - `lg` (Desktop): `1024px` and above, 12 columns, fixed container max `1200px` (`75rem`), `24px` gutters. Multi-column technical service catalog, team credibility modules, and integrated quotation engine.

Rhythm is deliberate: tight internal component padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`) ensures cards remain structural and dense, while generous vertical section spacing (`space-3xl` to `space-4xl`) provides breathing room between core value propositions, proof-of-work galleries, and contact channels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through structural layering and crisp, low-diffusion ambient shadows tinted with the primary navy hue rather than dirty grays:

- **Level 0 (Flat Ground)**: Background tone `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Surfaces)**: Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface accompanied by a crisp 1px perimeter border (`#E2E8F0`) and subtle contact shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Hover & Dropdowns)**: Elevated cards, service tiles on hover, and custom pickers: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.06)`. Border color darkens subtly to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky CTAs & Emergency Drawers)**: Floating WhatsApp trigger and persistent 24/7 bottom call bar on mobile: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08)`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Dialogs & Diagnostics Overlays)**: Rapid quote modals: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.16), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 41, 66, 0.08)` paired with an overlay backdrop of `#0F2942` at 60% opacity with a `4px` blur.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a **Soft (`roundedness: 1`)** geometric language. This communicates industrial rigor, architectural stability, and engineering discipline without feeling sterile or antiquated.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`)**: Applied to compact controls, input elements, inline technical tags, and emergency status badges.
- **Card & Surface Radius (`0.5rem` / `8px` - `rounded-lg`)**: Standard container rounding across diagnostic service cards, quotation panels, and testimonial containers.
- **Modal & Floating Element Radius (`0.75rem` / `12px` - `rounded-xl`)**: Applied to dialog overlays and floating bottom call sheets.
- **Pill Formats**: Restricted strictly to live availability indicators (e.g., the pulsating "Disponible Ahora" chip) and specialized mobile action icons to preserve contrast against the crisp rectangular UI framework.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Emergency Action (Secondary - Amber)**: Solid `#F59E0B` fill with `#0F172A` bold text. Used solely for immediate electrical assistance triggers. Hover switches to `#EA580C` with smooth 150ms transition.
- **Direct Dispatch (Tertiary - WhatsApp)**: Solid `#22C55E` fill with white text, featuring an official WhatsApp vector glyph. Fixed floating position on mobile at bottom right.
- **Corporate Primary (Navy)**: Solid `#0F2942` fill with white text for standard bookings, quote submissions, and technical sheet downloads.
- **Secondary Outline**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1.5px solid `#0F2942` stroke, `#0F2942` text.

### Badges & Emergency Chips
- Status badges feature a 2px live pulsating dot.
- Emergency 24/7 badge: `#FEF3C7` background, `#92400E` text, `#F59E0B` border.
- CFE & Certified Industrial badge: `#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, `#10B981` border.
- Typography is strictly `label-md` uppercase with `0.05em` tracking.

### Cards & Service Tiles
- Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface bordered by `#E2E8F0` with a top accent bar (3px height) matching the category type: `#0F2942` for residential, `#1E40AF` for industrial, and `#F59E0B` for emergency services.
- Incorporates explicit metadata: typical response time (e.g., "Respuesta: 30-45 min"), warranty length ("Garantía por escrito"), and certified technician indicator.

### Input Fields & Quotation Forms
- Clean white inputs with a 1px `#CBD5E1` border. Focus states transition crisply to a 2px `#0F2942` ring without fuzzy outer glows.
- Mobile form fields feature numeric keypad enforcement for telephone numbers and address inputs with auto-complete targeting the Puebla metropolitan zone.

### Sticky Conversion Bar (Mobile)
- A persistent bottom drawer docked at `bottom: 0` featuring a split CTA: one-tap direct phone call (`tel:`) styled in `#0F2942`, and direct emergency WhatsApp message styled in `#22C55E`. Ensures conversion touchpoints remain accessible regardless of scroll depth.
